Design a modern, premium, emotionally engaging, responsive homepage for an NGO called Brutality Free Community (BFC).

The organization is dedicated to street animal rescue, feeding, adoption, medical treatment, awareness campaigns, reflective collar drives, and community volunteering.

The website should not look like a traditional NGO website. Instead, it should feel like a premium nonprofit similar to Apple, Airbnb, Notion, or WWF with elegant typography, generous white space, soft shadows, rounded corners, glassmorphism, smooth curves, and subtle premium animations.

Overall Goal

Create a homepage that feels like a world-class nonprofit website, combining the visual quality of Apple, the cleanliness of Notion, the storytelling of Airbnb, and the emotional impact of WWF. The design should inspire trust, compassion, and action, encouraging visitors to donate, volunteer, adopt, or support the mission while maintaining a premium, modern, and highly polished aesthetic.
